## Summary

This project goal aims to establish a team charter for `t-rustup`, i.e. the
rustup team.

## Motivation

Having a charter for `t-rustup` would allow better transparency for both
`t-rustup` members and non-members.

On the one hand, for members, it would be beneficial to have clearly-defined
rules regarding the scope of this team's work, how should important decisions
be made within the team, as well as their general responsibilities.

On the other hand, for non-members and especially interested external
contributors, it would be helpful to have some concrete ideas regarding what
discussions are considered in-scope, as well as what is expected of them to
be able to join the team.

### The status quo

Following the [call for chartering work] in the Rust 2021 roadmap, the former
`wg-rustup` has made a draft version of its own
[charter](https://github.com/rust-lang/rustup/wiki/Rustup-working-group-charter).

[call for chartering work]: https://github.com/rust-lang/rfcs/blob/main/text/3037-roadmap-2021.md#chartering-work-in-the-rust-project

Unfortunately, however, this charter has never been put into use, and a lot has
changed since March 2021 , making this documentation rather outdated. Notably,
`wg-rustup` has become `t-rustup`, and none of its members at that time is
still on the team at the time of writing.

### What we propose to do about it

Replace the existing charter draft with a modernized one, placed under [the
rustup developer guide](https://rust-lang.github.io/rustup/dev-guide) as a
separate chapter for maximal visibility.
